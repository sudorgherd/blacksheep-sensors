# v0.2.0 Stage 6 — Local Receive Timing

**Status:** Complete — PASS
**Version:** `0.2.0-dev`
**Branch:** `v0.2.0-stage6-receive-timing`

## Objective, authority, and acceptance criteria

Stage 6 determines which local receive-time facts can be derived safely and
boundedly. It does not infer identity, movement, occupancy, mobility, behavior,
routes, dwell, or relationships between sources and does not claim cross-C5 or
cross-node synchronization.

ESP-IDF 5.5.4 `esp_wifi_he_types.h` declares `esp_wifi_rxctrl_t.timestamp` as
an unsigned 32-bit local packet-receive time in microseconds. The public comment
says it is precise only when modem sleep or light sleep is not enabled. It does
not guarantee continuity across Wi-Fi restart, deinitialization, reboot, sleep,
or other lifecycle boundaries. A natural unsigned wrap occurs every
4,294,967,296 microseconds (about 71.58 minutes), but host vectors rather than a
long physical wait are authoritative for wrap arithmetic.

The following criteria were fixed before final physical disposition:

1. the public timestamp is available and its 32-bit/microsecond contract is
   documented;
2. exact same-epoch modular arithmetic passes golden and injected-clock tests;
3. capture/lifecycle and channel boundaries create explicit discontinuities;
4. a known queue drop invalidates adjacency rather than being hidden;
5. repeated controlled Beacon windows produce coherent local deltas;
6. representative and higher ordinary owned-traffic runs remain bounded;
7. both C5 roles remain stable with documented queue, drop, callback, parser,
   shutdown, READY, and heartbeat evidence; and
8. no synchronization or behavioral-profile claim is introduced.

Candidate dispositions are ACCEPT, ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS, EXPERIMENTAL,
DEFER, or REJECT. No candidate is promoted merely because a field exists.

## Pure timing and epoch contract

`wifi_timing_state_t` is ESP-IDF-independent and fixed-size. It stores only the
current epoch, previous timestamp, observed global drop count, bounded event and
valid/invalid-delta counts, min/max/saturating sum, and discontinuity counters.
It retains no event list, address, per-source history, or payload.

For two valid timestamps in the same explicit epoch with unchanged drop count
and no channel boundary:

```text
delta_us = (uint32_t)(current_timestamp - previous_timestamp)
```

This is exact across one natural 32-bit wrap. Numerical decrease alone does not
start an epoch; lifecycle context does. Capture start creates a new nonzero
epoch. Reinitialization, receiver restart, and any future channel reconfigure
must likewise create an epoch or explicit channel boundary. No delta is derived
across unknown sleep/reset/restart continuity.

The first valid event, an invalid timestamp, epoch change, channel boundary, or
changed queue-drop count produces an invalid delta with an explicit reason.
After a drop, the first subsequently processed event becomes the new baseline;
the following event may again form a valid adjacent delta if no further
discontinuity occurs. This conservative worker snapshot can invalidate more
than the mathematically minimum adjacency, but never hides a known missing
queue event.

## Worker and window representation

The Stage 1 event already owned a copied `uint32_t timestamp_us`; Stage 6 adds no
callback fields or work. All timing arithmetic and aggregation remain in the
worker. The all-frame state observes successfully parsed events. A second
validation-only state observes only Beacons matched by the existing Stage 5
owned-transmitter and BSSID gate. The local real address header remains ignored,
is never printed, and no identifier appears in timing output.

Each finite firmware capture is one bounded activity window. Zero events yield
zero deltas; one event yields one first-sample invalid delta; window/capture
restart resets the state into a new epoch. Parser failures are counted by the
existing parser counters and never enter timing. Queue drops remain in Stage 1
and in Stage 6 discontinuity counts. The fixed-channel Stage 6 environments do
not change channel mid-window; a pure injected boundary proves future handling.

Summary serial output occurs only after promiscuous receive stops. It reports
aggregate timing, queue, parser, and callback results. No per-frame output is
added. The timestamp is assigned by the Wi-Fi receive path; worker and serial
scheduling latency are distinct and are not presented as timestamp jitter.

## Host evidence

The strict-warning host suite currently passes **1,045,013 assertions**. It
preserves all Stage 1–5 tests and 100,000 deterministic randomized parser inputs.
Stage 6 golden vectors cover equal timestamps, +1, large deltas,
`UINT32_MAX -> 0`, several wrap boundaries, invalid timestamps, first samples,
epoch mismatch, channel boundary, queue-drop discontinuity, state/window reset,
and saturating counters/sums. Injected monotonic, repeated, wrap, reset, drop,
and boundary sequences produced exact expected validity and deltas. MinGW still
lacks `libasan`/`libubsan`; the established strict-warning fallback passed.

## Physical method and evidence

Each assigned C5 uses its fixed qualified Stage 5 channel for finite eight-second
windows. Controlled periodic evidence uses only matched owned-AP Beacons.
Representative evidence uses ordinary received traffic. Burst evidence uses
ordinary higher-rate traffic on the owned WLAN, never jamming, deauthentication,
malformed injection, or third-party disruption. Every run records event and
delta counts, min/max/mean, discontinuities, queue high-water/drops, callback
timing, parser failures, shutdown, READY, and heartbeat.

### C5 #1 — 2.4 GHz

USB identity ending `9E:88` was rediscovered on a temporary local port. An
initial representative channel-5 window processed 3,707 of 3,707 enqueued
events with zero drops, queue high-water 2, 3,706 valid deltas and one
first-sample invalid delta. Delta min/max/mean were 0/49,601/2,141 us. Parser
failures and class mismatches were zero; callback min/max/mean were 6/50/6 us.
Shutdown drained cleanly and READY/heartbeat remained stable. The ignored local
selector was configured for the other AP radio, so controlled matches were zero;
this run is representative evidence, not periodic controlled evidence.

Three controlled periodic windows matched 78, 79, and 78 owned-AP Beacons and
produced 77, 78, and 77 valid deltas. Each window had one expected first-sample
invalid delta. Mean delta was exactly 102,401 us in all three windows; observed
min/max ranges were 88,732/116,071 us, 99,091/105,712 us, and
99,270/105,533 us. The wider first range is retained rather than discarded.
Every run had zero drops and zero drop/epoch/channel discontinuities, queue
high-water 1, 2, and 2, callback average 6 us, zero parser failures, clean
shutdown, and READY. No configured Beacon interval is claimed; the evidence
characterizes observed periodicity only.

A separately generated ordinary owned-WLAN burst window processed 3,153 of
3,153 enqueued events, with 3,152 valid deltas, one expected first-sample
invalid delta, and delta min/max/mean 0/72,672/2,539 us. Queue high-water was
2; drops, discontinuities, parser failures, and class mismatches were zero;
callback min/max/mean was 5/51/6 us. The controlled periodic stream remained
coherent during the burst: 78 matched Beacons, 77 valid deltas, and
99,337/105,273/102,372 us min/max/mean. Shutdown drained cleanly and READY plus
heartbeat remained stable. These counts characterize bounded load only and are
not interpreted as behavior.

### C5 #2 — 5 GHz

Three controlled periodic channel-48 windows matched 79, 78, and 73 owned-AP
Beacons. The first two produced 78 and 77 valid deltas with min/max/mean

not classified as a timestamp defect. Each window had only the expected
first-sample invalid delta, zero timing discontinuity counters, queue high-water

A separate representative window processed 518 of 518 events, with 517 valid
deltas, one expected first-sample invalid delta, and 149/56,007/15,364 us

102,292/102,511/102,401 us. Queue high-water was 1; drops, parser failures,
class mismatches, and discontinuities were zero; callback min/max/mean was
6/51/6 us. Shutdown and READY/heartbeat were stable.

A separately generated ordinary owned-WLAN burst window processed 571 of 571
events, with 570 valid deltas, one expected first-sample invalid delta, and

## Provisional dispositions and open work

The both-board periodic, representative, and burst matrices, complete host
regression, and complete firmware matrix pass. Stage 6 therefore passes its
acceptance gate. Current implementation and physical evidence support:

| Candidate | Current disposition | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| local receive timestamp | ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS | local 32-bit microseconds; no sleep/lifecycle continuity claim |
| local inter-arrival delta | ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS | same explicit epoch, no known drop/channel boundary |
| bounded event count/window | ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS | finite aggregate only; not a behavior fingerprint |
| periodic-arrival characterization | ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS | local controlled-source aggregates; delivery gaps remain visible and no configured interval is inferred |

Natural physical wrap is unobserved and optional because exact host wrap vectors
pass. No production window size, activity profile, persistent timing history,
or Stage 7 PHY/rate work is implemented. Stage 5 remains provisionally
checkpointed with its outstanding qualification deferred to Stage 9.

The isolated 512,014 us 5 GHz controlled-source arrival gap remains an honest
delivery-coverage limitation: it occurred without a BLACKSHEEP queue drop and
is not converted into an assumed missing-frame count. Timestamp behavior across
sleep remains unqualified because Stage 6 did not enable sleep. These do not
invalidate the bounded local timing substrate or its explicit continuity rules.
