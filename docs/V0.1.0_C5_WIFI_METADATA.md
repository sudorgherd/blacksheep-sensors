# ESP32-C5 Promiscuous Receive Metadata Inventory

This document records the v0.1.0 capability boundary for ESP32-C5 revision 1.2
with ESP-IDF 5.5.4. It inventories metadata exposed directly by the public
promiscuous receive callback. It does not select BLACKSHEEP sensor attributes,
define an observation record, or interpret raw 802.11 frame bytes; those are
v0.2.0 concerns.

## Authority and classification

ESP32-C5 enables `CONFIG_SOC_WIFI_HE_SUPPORT` and
`SOC_WIFI_MAC_VERSION_NUM=3`. Consequently, public `wifi_pkt_rx_ctrl_t` is an
alias of the packed `esp_wifi_rxctrl_t` defined in
`components/esp_wifi/include/esp_wifi_he_types.h`. The callback buffer is
`wifi_promiscuous_pkt_t`, whose first member is this receive-control structure,
and its separate `wifi_promiscuous_pkt_type_t` argument supplies the packet
class.

Statuses mean:

* **Verified available:** documented field produced plausible real-RF values.
* **Exposed but not observed:** documented field was present, but this bounded
  sample did not demonstrate its useful/non-default state.
* **Conditional validity:** documentation limits meaning to a PHY, frame, sleep,
  width, validity flag, or success condition.
* **Not exposed directly:** requires parsing raw frame bytes or another API.
* **Undocumented/ambiguous:** storage is public, but documented semantics or the
  bounded observations are insufficient for reliance.

## Direct ESP-IDF metadata surface

All `wifi_pkt_rx_ctrl_t` fields below are present on both C5 band roles. Band
columns report this task's physical evidence, not a claim that a field is
useful to BLACKSHEEP.

| Metadata | C type / width | Documented meaning and validity | 2.4 GHz | 5 GHz | v0.2.0 follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| callback `type` | `wifi_promiscuous_pkt_type_t` enum | Driver class: management, control, data, or miscellaneous | Verified available: management | Verified available: management and data | Decide whether callback class is sufficiently stable/useful |
| `rssi` | signed bit-field, 8 bits | Reception-frame RSSI | Verified available: -41 to -33 dBm | Verified available: -83 to -52 dBm | Assess reliability and aggregation |
| `rate` | unsigned bit-field, 5 bits | 11b transmit rate when `cur_bb_format` is 11b; otherwise L-SIG Rate | Conditional validity: value 0 with 11b format | Conditional validity: value 11 with 11a/11g format | Map encodings only after deciding their value |
| `rxmatch0..3` | four unsigned 1-bit fields | Whether the frame matches driver interfaces 0 through 3 | Exposed; observed combined pattern 9 | Exposed; observed combined pattern 8 | Interface identity/semantics are ambiguous for sensing |
| `he_siga1` | `uint32_t` | Raw HE-SIGA1, HT-SIG, or VHT-SIG depending on PHY format | Exposed but zero in sample | Exposed but zero in sample | Decoding would be conditional PHY analysis |

The structure contains reserved bit fields which are not metadata and were not
read. ESP32-C5's HE-capable public structure does not directly expose decoded
MCS, coding, aggregation, STBC, guard interval, antenna selection, or an
explicit band field. Some PHY information may be encoded in raw SIG words, but
decoding those words is not equivalent to a directly decoded driver field and
was not attempted here. The configured band and primary channel remain
available through the existing Wi-Fi control APIs.

| Candidate metadata | Classification | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explicit Wi-Fi band | Not exposed directly in receive control | Known from `esp_wifi_get_band_mode()` and channel configuration, not supplied per packet |
| Decoded MCS, coding, aggregation, STBC, or guard interval | Not exposed directly by the C5 public receive-control structure | May require conditional decoding of raw SIG words; deferred |
| Receive antenna | Not exposed directly by the C5 public receive-control structure | The older non-HE structure has an antenna bit, but the C5 HE structure does not |
| Frame subtype | Not exposed directly | Requires reading the raw 802.11 frame-control bytes |
| Source/destination/BSSID and OUI | Not exposed directly | Requires raw header parsing and, for OUI, further derivation |
| SSID/information elements | Not exposed directly | Requires management-frame body parsing |
| Sequence, retry, and protected-frame semantics | Not exposed directly | Requires raw frame-control/sequence-control parsing |

## Raw-frame and other-API boundary

`wifi_promiscuous_pkt_t.payload` contains raw data or management-frame bytes,
but this task never read, copied, printed, or retained it. Source/destination
MAC addresses, BSSID, OUI/vendor, SSID/information elements, sequence and retry
semantics, protected-frame indication, frame subtype, and detailed 802.11
header meaning therefore remain **not exposed directly** by receive control.
They require raw-frame parsing and are deferred to v0.2.0.

CSI is a separate subsystem/API and was not enabled. Its buffers, address
fields, payload pointers, and receive sequence information are not part of this
promiscuous receive-control characterization.

## Physical method and evidence

On 2026-08-25, dedicated `c5_metadata_24ghz` and `c5_metadata_5ghz`
environments used the existing management/control/data filter, one fixed
channel, and an eight-second window. The callback only updated fixed-size
counters, ranges, and seen-value masks under a critical section. Summary output
occurred after promiscuous mode was disabled.

C5 #1, USB serial `10:BD:A3:F3:9E:88` on temporary COM6, explicitly verified
2.4-GHz-only mode and channel 1. Upload/hash verification, the ESP-IDF 5.5.4
second stage, ESP32-C5 revision 1.2, QIO/80 MHz 32 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM, and the
PSRAM test passed. Four management callbacks were observed. Promiscuous and
Wi-Fi shutdown passed, followed by heartbeats 0–34 without reset, panic, crash,
watchdog, or resource-exhaustion symptoms.

C5 #2, USB serial `10:BD:A3:F3:AC:6C` on temporary COM7, explicitly applied the
manual US domain, verified 5-GHz-only mode, and used non-DFS channel 36. The
same upload, boot, flash, PSRAM, and memory-test baseline passed. The window
observed 190 callbacks: 157 management and 33 data. Promiscuous and Wi-Fi
shutdown passed, followed by heartbeats 0–31 without reset, panic, crash,
watchdog, or resource-exhaustion symptoms.

The observed differences describe these bounded RF samples, not guarantees
about either band. Broader traffic-format coverage, field reliability, and
selection of useful BLACKSHEEP attributes belong to v0.2.0.
