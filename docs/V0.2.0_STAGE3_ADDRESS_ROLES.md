# v0.2.0 Stage 3 — Wi-Fi Address Roles

**Status:** Complete
**Version:** `0.2.0-dev`
**Scope:** address-slot extraction, protocol-defined roles, address-bit
classification, and driver group-bit comparison only

## Objective and authority

Stage 3 answers which complete six-byte raw address slots are present and which
receiver (RA), transmitter (TA), destination (DA), source (SA), and BSSID roles
IEEE 802.11 permits the parser to assign. It does not identify a device.

The implementation follows IEEE 802.11-2020 MAC header semantics, the public
Linux `include/linux/ieee80211.h` DS matrix and frame structures, Linux
`net/wireless/util.c` address conversion, and the installed ESP-IDF 5.5.4
public C5 `esp_wifi_rxctrl_t` definition for `is_group`. Raw Addr1–Addr4 slots
are structural facts; semantic roles are context-dependent facts. One slot may
supply multiple roles, and unavailable roles remain invalid.

Public references:

* <https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/master/include/linux/ieee80211.h>
* <https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/master/net/wireless/util.c>

## Bounded representation and extraction

Each raw slot owns `uint8_t octets[6]` plus explicit validity. Each semantic
role owns a copied six-byte value, validity, and its source-slot enum. There are
no strings, heap objects, or pointers into a capture event. Addr1/2/3 offsets
are 4/10/16; Addr4 is 24 and is read only when the Stage 2 descriptor requires
it. A partial address is never exposed. Parser failure returns no raw or
semantic validity.

Management frames map Addr1 to RA/DA, Addr2 to TA/SA, and Addr3 to BSSID for
every Stage 2-supported ordinary management subtype. Reserved subtypes remain
unparsed.

The complete data matrix is:

| To DS | From DS | Addr1 | Addr2 | Addr3 | Addr4 | BSSID |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | RA, DA | TA, SA | BSSID | absent | Addr3 |
| 1 | 0 | RA, BSSID | TA, SA | DA | absent | Addr1 |
| 0 | 1 | RA, DA | TA, BSSID | SA | absent | Addr2 |
| 1 | 1 | RA | TA | DA | SA | unknown |

QoS and HT Control change length, not this matrix. Four-address/WDS is fully
host-validated; no BSSID is invented.

Control semantics are subtype-specific:

| Subtype | Roles |
| --- | --- |
| Control Wrapper | Addr1=RA; carried frame is not interpreted |
| BAR / compressed BA | Addr1=RA, Addr2=TA |
| PS-Poll | Addr1=RA/BSSID, Addr2=TA |
| RTS | Addr1=RA, Addr2=TA |
| CTS / ACK | Addr1=RA |
| CF-End / CF-End+CF-Ack | Addr1=RA, Addr2=BSSID |

SA/DA are not assigned to control frames. Stage 2-unsupported Trigger,
Control Extension, Multi-TID, and oversized Basic BA variants receive no
Stage 3 semantics.

## Classification and driver comparison

Classification precedence is invalid, all-ones broadcast, I/G-bit group, then
individual. Only individual addresses are split by the U/L bit into globally
or locally administered. `LOCAL_INDIVIDUAL` is a bit property, not evidence
that privacy randomization produced an arbitrary address. The physical source
was independently configured with a local address, so only that controlled
source is known to exercise the local-admin case.

Driver `rx_ctrl.is_group` is copied as one event flag in the callback and
compared with parsed Addr1 in the worker. Counters distinguish individual
agreement, group agreement, broadcast with driver-group, disagreement, and
unavailable comparison. Driver metadata never replaces parsed Addr1. Default
output is aggregate-only; no identifier diagnostic mode or MAC logger was
needed or added.

## Host tests and bounds

The strict C11 suite passed **350,694 assertions**. It covers every supported
management subtype, all 64 data subtype/DS combinations (including QoS, Addr4,
and HT Control), every supported control semantic mapping, exact role aliasing,
classification edge cases, driver-group outcomes, Stage 1 queue/copy behavior,
Stage 2 layout/truncation behavior, and address-boundary failure. It also
passed 100,000 deterministic randomized inputs of lengths 0–40 with invariants
that every valid role names a valid raw slot and owns identical bytes.

MinGW still lacks `libasan` and `libubsan`; the unchanged sanitizer link
limitation falls back to `-std=c11 -Wall -Wextra -Werror -pedantic -O2`.

## Controlled physical evidence

Both finite eight-second windows used fixed assigned bands/channels. The other
owned C5 transmitted a synthetic 1000-byte three-address data frame with a
broadcast Addr1 and an independently configured locally administered source.
Owned infrastructure supplied ordinary management/data and natural group or
individual traffic. Expected values were known privately from configuration;
only role/class aggregates were emitted. No address, SSID, payload, pcap, or
serial log is retained in the repository.

### C5 #1 — 2.4 GHz

USB identity ended `9E:88`; temporary port was COM6. Channel 1 and 2.4-GHz-only
mode were verified. Upload hashes, ESP-IDF boot, revision 1.2, QIO/80 MHz,
32 MB flash, and 8 MB PSRAM test passed.

The final window produced 738 callbacks/enqueues/processed events, high-water
1, and zero drops. All 738 layouts resolved semantics; roles RA/TA/DA/SA/BSSID
were each valid 738 times. Layouts were 7 management beacons and 731 ordinary
data frames. Addr1 classification was 738 broadcast; every driver comparison
was broadcast-plus-group with zero disagreement. Semantic source classification
was 3 global individual and 735 local individual, establishing the controlled
local source without printing it. Parser/class errors were zero. Callback
timing was 5/49/6 microseconds minimum/maximum/average. Shutdown drained cleanly,
READY returned, and nine heartbeats followed.

### C5 #2 — 5 GHz

USB identity ended `AC:6C`; temporary port was COM7. Manual US context,
channel 36, and 5-GHz-only mode were verified with the same hardware baseline.

The final window produced 927 callbacks/enqueues/processed events, high-water
1, and zero drops. All 927 layouts resolved semantics; roles RA/TA/DA/SA/BSSID
were each valid 927 times. Observed management subtypes were beacon (150) and
action (1); data subtypes were basic (771), QoS data (1), and QoS Null (4).
Addr1 was 905 broadcast, 16 group, and 6 local individual. Driver comparison
recorded 905 broadcast-plus-group, 16 group agreements, 6 individual
agreements, and zero disagreement/unavailable. Sources were 90 global and 837
local individuals. Parser/class errors were zero. Callback timing was 6/51/6
microseconds. Shutdown drained cleanly, READY returned, and nine heartbeats
followed.

After the complete build matrix, both boards were restored to their final
Stage 3 sensor environments and observed simultaneously for 18 additional
heartbeats each. This confirmed stable post-flash operation without retaining
the serial output.

Control roles and four-address/WDS remain **HOST VALIDATED / PHYSICALLY NOT
OBSERVED**. Both ordinary DS directions were not separately forced over RF;
the complete matrix is exhaustively host-validated. Physical absence is not
treated as failure.
